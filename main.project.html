Bhutan is a small, beautiful country in the Eastern Himalayas, known for its rich culture, peaceful environment, and Gross National Happiness philosophy. - Capital: Thimphu - Official language: Dzongkha - Currency: Bhutanese Ngultrum (Nu.) - National animal: Takin - National bird: Raven - National flower: Blue poppy - National sport: Archery - King: His Majesty King Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck - Location: Between India and China - Famous for: Monasteries, dzongs, mountains, traditional festivals, and Gross National Happiness.
Bhutan is a beautiful country with rich culture, peaceful people, amazing landscapes, and historic places. It is a special destination for people who want to experience nature and Bhutanese culture.
